How scoring works

Same seven habits, same one point each, so 49 is a perfect week. The targets behind each habit come from your own plan, so a point means the same effort for both of you.

Today's movement is whatever your plan says for that day. If you're run down, the recovery swap listed under it still earns the point. Sleep always beats a hard workout.

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Whoever has fewer points on Sunday night plans date night for the winner.

Treats on plan means nothing sweet or fried outside your bank of 3. Chips and cookies stay out of the house.
